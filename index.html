About
Everything runs in this browser tab — no audio or text leaves the device.

SmolLM2-135M is tiny on purpose for speed. It's set to reply short and fast (greedy decoding, ~24 tokens max) rather than "think" — replies may be blunt, odd, or nonsensical. That's expected, not a bug.

Models are cached by the browser after first download (IndexedDB / OPFS), so this is a one-time cost per device/browser.
